The problem

Manual sampling tells you what the water was like, not what it is like now

One lab test a week captures a single moment. Everything that happens between samples stays invisible — until it surfaces as a complaint, an inspection finding or a failure.

One sample a week

A lab result is a point in the past. A turbidity spike or an oxygen crash between sampling rounds simply never shows up.

History in three places

Site logbooks, an engineer's spreadsheet, paper certificates. Pulling together one year for one intake is a day's work.

Alerts after the fact

By the time anyone notices, the plume has moved downstream. That leaves less time to react — and to explain.

How it works

From electrode to dashboard in four steps

You don't have to replace your instruments. The platform takes readings from any sensor with a standard interface and turns them into a picture you can act on.

  1. 01

    Sensors in the water

    pH, turbidity, dissolved oxygen, temperature, conductivity. Submersible probes or flow-through cells, depending on the site.

  2. 02

    Logger and connectivity

    Readings are collected and sent over NB-IoT, LTE-M or LoRaWAN. Mains or solar power, with a buffer for connectivity gaps.

  3. 03

    Cloud

    MQTT ingestion, time-series storage, assets and monitoring points, calibration log.

  4. 04

    Dashboard and alerts

    Web interface, threshold rules, notifications by email, SMS or webhook, reports and an API for your own systems.

We work with sensors that speak SDI-12, Modbus RTU or 4–20 mA. If the instruments are already on site, we connect them — we don't sell a replacement.

Sensors

What we measure

The baseline set is pH, turbidity and temperature. Everything else is added to suit the site: ion-selective electrodes, level sensors, flow meters.

pH

pH units

Acidity and alkalinity. The baseline parameter for any monitoring point.

Turbidity

NTU

Suspended solids and silt. A fast signal of runoff, discharge or filter performance.

Dissolved oxygen

mg/l

The best single indicator of a water body's health and of treatment performance.

Temperature

°C

Drives gas solubility, reaction rates and biological activity.

Conductivity

µS/cm

Mineralisation. Saline discharges and shock loads show up immediately.

ORP

mV

Redox potential. Used to verify that disinfection is working.

Ammonium

mg/l

A marker of domestic sewage and of organic matter breaking down.

Level

m

Flooding, overflow and protection of the intake screen.

The platform

More than charts: a working tool for the duty officer and the engineer

Data has to reach the person who makes the decision, in a form they can act on — not as a thousand-row export.

Asset map

Every monitoring point on one map: status, time of last contact, current readings and deviations.

Threshold rules

A separate rule set for each point: value, duration, time of day. Alerts go to the duty officer, not to a general channel.

History and charts

Time series with no gaps: zoom to any period, overlay parameters, compare monitoring points against each other.

Reports and exports

A monthly report for a site in Excel or CSV, with periods, gaps and calibration marks.

Calibration log

Calibration and verification dates are tied to the individual sensor, so you know when a reading can be trusted.

API and integrations

REST API and webhooks to push data into SCADA, asset systems or your own reporting.

Where it fits

Four typical jobs

Water intakes

Don't put water into supply that you'll have to explain later

Turbidity and pH at the inlet, and again after treatment. You see the source shift, not the complaints a day later.

Rivers and reservoirs

Points above and below a discharge

Two points instead of one: the difference shows what a specific source contributes, rather than the river's average.

Aquaculture

Oxygen in the pens, before the stock loss

Overnight oxygen sags and temperature swings, flagged while there is still time to start aeration.

Industrial effluent

Outfall and compliance point

A continuous record at the outfall and at the compliance point, and a log that feeds the site's reporting without manual reconciliation.
